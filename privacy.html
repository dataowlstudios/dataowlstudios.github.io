Patang: A Kite's Journey

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Patang: A Kite's Journey, an Android mobile game developed and published by Data Owl Studios ("we", "our", or "us").

We value your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is collected, used, and protected when you play our game.

This Privacy Policy applies to Patang: A Kite's Journey and its associated Google Play Games Services features.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require players to directly provide personal details such as their full name, email address, or phone number to Data Owl Studios.

However, the game integrates third-party services that may automatically collect certain information.

This information may include:

  • Device information, including device model, operating system, and identifiers.
  • App usage information, including gameplay interactions and app performance data.
  • Advertising information, including advertising identifiers and ad interactions.
  • Approximate location information, such as country or region, inferred by service providers. We do not require precise GPS location for gameplay.
  • Google Play Games profile information when you use supported Play Games features.

3. Google Play Games Services

Patang: A Kite's Journey integrates Google Play Games Services to support Google account authentication and connected gaming features.

When you sign in using Google Play Games, Google handles the authentication process. Depending on the features enabled, the game may access information such as:

  • Your Google Play Games player ID.
  • Your Play Games display name.
  • Your Play Games profile image.
  • Gameplay-related information needed for supported features, such as achievements or leaderboards.

We use information received through Google Play Games Services only to authenticate players and provide supported gaming features.

We do not use Google Play Games profile information for advertising targeting or sell that information to advertisers.

Google may independently process account and gameplay information under its own privacy policies.

You can learn more here: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Patang: A Kite's Journey uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Advertisement impressions and interactions.
  • Approximate location information.
  • App usage and diagnostic information.

Advertising-related information may be used to deliver ads, measure advertising performance, detect fraud, and support the security and operation of advertising services.

Rewarded advertisements are displayed when players choose to use a feature that offers an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Google and its advertising partners may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

For more information, visit: Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising Mediation Services

Where enabled in the distributed version of the game, advertising mediation services such as Unity LevelPlay may facilitate the delivery of advertisements through supported advertising networks.

These services and their advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising information, app interactions, and related technical data for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Additional information about Unity's privacy practices is available at: Unity Privacy Policy .

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the game and its integrated services may be used to:

  • Authenticate players using Google Play Games Services.
  • Provide supported gameplay features.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Monitor app performance and investigate technical problems.
  • Improve gameplay and user experience.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, or misuse.

7. Data Sharing

Data Owl Studios does not sell the personal information it receives through Patang: A Kite's Journey.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers involved in delivering authentication, gaming, advertising, and related technical services.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or valid legal process.

8. Data Storage and Security

Some gameplay information, such as local progress and preferences, may be stored on your device.

Data processed by Google Play Games Services, Google AdMob, and other integrated providers may be stored and managed on their respective systems.

We seek to use reasonable safeguards to protect information within our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay information may remain on your device until you clear the game's app data or uninstall it.

Third-party services may retain information in accordance with their own data retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

If you would like to request information about personal data held directly by Data Owl Studios, or request deletion of such information, contact us at:

dataowlstudios@gmail.com

We will review applicable requests and delete eligible information within our control, subject to legal obligations.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.
